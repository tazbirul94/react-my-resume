# My Resume — Interactive Portfolio & Resume Website

A personal resume website that goes beyond a static PDF. Built as a live, interactive portfolio where visitors can explore your professional story in a polished, modern interface.

---

## What It Looks Like

The site is a single-page experience divided into clearly organized sections, each designed to tell a different part of your professional story.

---

## Features

### Hero Landing Section
The first thing visitors see is a bold, animated intro — your name, job title, and a quick summary. Links to your GitHub, LinkedIn, email, and other profiles are right there. Two buttons guide visitors to either explore your resume or get in touch directly.

### About Me
A dedicated section with your profile photo, a written bio, and key contact details — email, phone, location, and website — all clickable. A badge shows whether you're currently open to new opportunities.

### Skills
Your technical skills are organized into color-coded groups — for example, Backend, Frontend, Cloud, and Data. Each skill appears as a clean tag inside its group, making it easy to scan at a glance.

### Work Experience
A visual timeline of your career. Each company gets its own card showing all the roles you held there, how long you were there, and what you accomplished. Key achievements are highlighted. If you have a long career history, older positions are hidden by default with a "Show more" button.

### Portfolio / Projects
A grid of project cards. Each card shows a thumbnail image, the project name, its category, and the technologies used. Clicking a project opens it in a new tab.

### Education
Your degrees and institutions listed with dates, GPA (supports both US and German grading scales), and any thesis or academic highlights.

### Certifications
A card for each certification showing the issuing organization, date, and a direct link to verify the credential online.

### Languages
All languages you speak, displayed with CEFR proficiency levels (A1 through C2, or Mother Tongue).

### Interests & Hobbies
A simple, friendly section showing what you enjoy outside of work.

### Testimonials / References
Quotes from colleagues, managers, or clients displayed as reference cards. A mailto link lets visitors request a formal reference letter directly.

---

## Personalization Options

### Dark Mode & Light Mode
A toggle in the navigation bar switches between a clean white (Apple-inspired) theme and a dark navy theme. Your preference is saved so it persists between visits.

### Two Languages
The entire site is available in **English** and **German**. Visitors can switch languages using the EN / DE buttons in the navigation bar. All section labels and UI text translate instantly.

### Download as PDF
A button in the navigation bar lets visitors download your resume as a PDF using their browser's built-in print dialog. The printed version is formatted cleanly — no navigation bars, no buttons, just the content.

---

## Admin Panel

Behind a login, there's a full content management panel where you can edit everything on the site without touching any code:

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Basics | Edit your name, title, bio, photo, and contact details |
| Work | Add, edit, or delete jobs, roles, highlights, and skills |
| Education | Manage degrees, institutions, dates, and GPA |
| Skills | Create skill groups and add/remove individual skills |
| Languages | Add languages with proficiency levels |
| Interests | Add interest categories and keywords |
| Projects | Manage portfolio cards with images and links |
| Certifications | Add certifications with verification links |
| Testimonials | Add or remove reference quotes |
| Locales | Enable or disable language versions of the site |

All changes are saved to a database and reflected on the live site immediately.

---

## Responsive Design

The site works on every screen size. On mobile, the navigation collapses into a hamburger menu. Grids reflow to single columns. Buttons and links are touch-friendly.
